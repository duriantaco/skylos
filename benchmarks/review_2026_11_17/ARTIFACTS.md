revisions, manifests, compact result summaries, regression fixtures and
reproduction scripts in Git. Full scan outputs, cloned targets, caches and
repeated diagnostic arms are local artifacts excluded by `.gitignore`.

`~/Desktop/skylos-benchmark-2026-11-14/review_2026_11_17/report/`.

portable conclusions also have versioned summaries and source hashes.
