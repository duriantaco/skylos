A practical review flow

  1. State the intended behavior. Write a concrete input and its expected result. Identify what must stay unchanged.
  2. Follow the change through the system. Read the complete function, its callers, the helpers it uses, and the before/after code.
  3. Challenge the implementation. Check boundary inputs, failure handling, shared state, compatibility, and complexity.
  4. Inspect the tests. Check that their assertions are meaningful and would fail for a broken implementation. Run the relevant checks; a static test link is not coverage.
  5. Use AI to explain and propose concerns. Verify its claims against source and tests. AI can miss defects and report issues that are not real.
  6. Leave actionable findings. Name the location, triggering input, actual consequence, and the change or test needed. State what was checked and what remains unverified.

Sources: Google review guidance · GitHub's guidance on AI limitations
